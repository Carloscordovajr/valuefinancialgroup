Conventional Loans in Arizona

Not government-backed, not one-size-fits-all: conventional financing compared across 20+ lenders so you get the terms that actually fit your goals.

Apply in 5 mins Schedule a Consultation (623) 755-7528

A conventional loan is a mortgage that isn't insured or guaranteed by a government agency like the FHA or VA. Instead, it's underwritten to standard guidelines set by the conventional market, which is why it's one of the most widely used loan types for Arizona homebuyers and move-up buyers alike.

In competitive Arizona markets, conventional financing often appeals to sellers too: in multiple-offer situations, a conventional offer can sometimes be viewed as more straightforward than government-backed financing, depending on the property and appraisal considerations. Because every lender sets its own pricing, credit overlays, and down payment flexibility within conventional guidelines, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders can meaningfully change your terms.

Mortgage Insurance

How Conventional PMI Actually Works.

Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is often required on a conventional loan when your down payment falls below a certain threshold. Unlike some loan types where mortgage insurance lasts for the life of the loan, conventional PMI can typically be removed once you reach a sufficient equity position, either automatically or by request.

We'll walk through exactly how PMI applies to your loan and what it takes to have it removed down the road.
