FHA Loans

FHA Loans in Arizona

Flexible, government-backed financing compared across 20+ lenders, including county-specific loan limits and down payment assistance pairing most national lenders don't walk you through.

Apply in 5 mins Schedule a Consultation (623) 755-7528

FHA loans are government-backed mortgages designed to help qualified buyers purchase a home with more flexible credit and down payment guidelines than many conventional programs. They've long been a popular entry point for first-time buyers, but they're used by move-up buyers and repeat buyers across Arizona too.

Because FHA requirements and lender overlays vary, and because loan limits themselves are set on a county-by-county basis, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders can change both what you qualify for and how smoothly your purchase moves through underwriting.

Arizona-Specific

FHA Loan Limits Vary by Arizona County.

Unlike a single nationwide number, FHA loan limits are set county by county, and Arizona's counties don't all share the same limit. Where your property is located can directly affect how much you're able to finance with FHA.

Because these figures are updated annually by HUD, we'll confirm the current limit for your specific county as part of your consultation rather than relying on a number that may already be out of date.

Mortgage Insurance

FHA Mortgage Insurance (MIP), Explained.

FHA loans require mortgage insurance premiums (MIP), which work differently than conventional PMI. Depending on your down payment, MIP can remain in place for a longer portion of the loan term rather than dropping off automatically at a set equity level. That's an important distinction from conventional financing, and one we'll walk through clearly so you know exactly what to expect, including your options for refinancing out of FHA mortgage insurance down the road.

Pairing Opportunities

FHA + Down Payment Assistance.

Arizona offers county and state-level down payment assistance programs that can pair with FHA financing, helping reduce what you need to bring to closing. Program availability, funding, and eligibility change over time and often depend on which county you're buying in, so rather than quoting figures that may already be outdated, we'll review current programs together and confirm what you actually qualify for.
