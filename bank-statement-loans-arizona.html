Bank Statement Loans in Arizona

Qualify for a mortgage using your bank statements instead of tax returns — built for self-employed borrowers, business owners, and gig-economy workers whose income doesn't fit a traditional paystub.

A bank statement loan is a mortgage option built for borrowers whose income doesn't translate neatly onto a W-2 or a standard tax return. Instead of qualifying you using your reported taxable income, lenders review your personal or business bank statements to understand your actual cash flow — which can tell a very different story than a tax return built around deductions.

That makes bank statement financing a common fit for self-employed professionals, small business owners, freelancers, and gig-economy workers whose write-offs reduce their income on paper, even when their real earnings support a mortgage comfortably. Because documentation requirements, income-calculation methods, and eligible property types vary widely by lender, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders is especially important here.

A Common Question

Not the Same as a "Stated Income" Loan.

Bank statement loans are sometimes confused with the stated-income, no-documentation products from before 2008. The difference matters: bank statement loans still verify your income — just through real deposit history in your accounts, rather than a tax return. Your cash flow is documented, reviewed, and underwritten; it's simply measured differently than a traditional W-2 file.
