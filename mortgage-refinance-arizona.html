Refinancing generally falls into one of two categories. A rate-and-term refinance adjusts your interest rate or loan term without meaningfully changing your loan balance beyond closing costs. It's about improving your current terms. A cash-out refinance replaces your existing mortgage with a larger loan, letting you access a portion of your home's built-up equity as cash for other goals.

Arizona's home values have seen meaningful appreciation in recent years, which has left many homeowners with real equity to work with. Whichever path fits your goals, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders can meaningfully change the terms you end up with.

The Real Question

Is Refinancing Actually Worth It?

The honest answer comes down to your break-even point: the moment your accumulated monthly savings equal what you paid in closing costs. The formula is simple: divide your total closing costs by your monthly payment savings. The result tells you, in months, how long it takes before refinancing starts actually saving you money.

From there, it's a question of how long you plan to stay in the home. We'll run this math with your real numbers before you decide, not a rate advertised to someone else's scenario.
