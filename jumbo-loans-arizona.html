Jumbo Loans in Arizona

A jumbo loan is a mortgage that exceeds the conforming loan limit set annually by the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA). Conforming loans are underwritten to a standard set of guidelines that apply broadly across lenders; jumbo loans aren't. That means each lender in our network sets its own rules for credit, down payment, reserves, and documentation on jumbo financing.

That difference is exactly why comparing lenders matters more on a jumbo loan than almost any other loan type. Two lenders can look at the same borrower and the same property and arrive at very different terms. Rather than applying with a single bank and hoping their guidelines fit your situation, we shop your scenario across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders to find the program structured around how you actually qualify.

What Lenders Look At

Jumbo Underwriting Looks at the Full Picture.

Because jumbo loans fall outside conforming guidelines, lenders weigh these factors more heavily than on a conventional loan.

Credit Profile

Your credit history and score influence which jumbo programs and lenders you qualify with.

Down Payment
