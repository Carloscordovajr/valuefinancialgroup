An ITIN is a tax processing number issued by the IRS to individuals who need to file a U.S. tax return but aren't eligible for a Social Security number. An ITIN loan uses that number, paired with alternative documentation of your income and credit history, to open a path to homeownership that a Social-Security-number-based program like FHA simply doesn't offer.

We believe everyone deserves a clear, respectful path to homeownership. ITIN loan programs are a legitimate, established part of the mortgage market, and because documentation standards and terms vary meaningfully from lender to lender, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders can make a real difference in what you qualify for.

Why ITIN Loans Exist

FHA Requires an SSN. ITIN Loans Don't.

Government-backed programs like FHA require a Social Security number, which leaves out borrowers who file taxes using an ITIN. ITIN loan programs were built to fill that gap, giving borrowers a documented, legitimate way to qualify for financing using the identification and income records they do have.

We'll walk you through exactly how an ITIN loan compares to other programs so you understand your full set of options.
