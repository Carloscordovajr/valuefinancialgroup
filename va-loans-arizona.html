VA Loan Benefits

Why Veterans & Service Members Choose VA Financing.

No Down Payment Required

Eligible borrowers with full entitlement can finance a home purchase with no down payment.

No Monthly Mortgage Insurance

VA loans don't require monthly private mortgage insurance (PMI), unlike many conventional and FHA programs.

Competitive Rates

VA-backed financing often comes with competitive pricing compared to conventional options.

Flexible Credit Guidelines

The VA itself sets no minimum credit score, though individual lenders do — comparing lenders gives you more paths to approval.

Closing Cost Limits

The VA limits certain closing costs borrowers can be charged, which can help keep your out-of-pocket costs predictable.

A Reusable Benefit

VA loan eligibility isn't a one-time benefit — many veterans can use it again for a future home purchase.

VA Jumbo Financing

Full Entitlement Means No VA Loan Limit.

For eligible veterans with full entitlement, the VA does not cap the loan amount you can finance — the limiting factor becomes the lender's own guidelines, not a government-set ceiling. That opens the door to financing above typical conforming loan amounts using VA backing, often called VA jumbo financing.

Because every lender sets its own requirements for larger VA loan amounts, comparing options across our network matters even more for higher-value purchases and refinances.
