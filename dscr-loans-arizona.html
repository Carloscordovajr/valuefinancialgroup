DSCR Loans in Arizona

Qualify using your property's rental income instead of your personal income, compared across 20+ lenders so Arizona investors get a program built around their portfolio, not their tax returns.

A DSCR loan (short for debt-service coverage ratio) is an investment property mortgage that looks at the property, not the person. Instead of qualifying you with personal income, tax returns, and a debt-to-income calculation, lenders evaluate whether the rental income a property generates can reasonably cover its own mortgage payment, including principal, interest, taxes, insurance, and any association dues.

That structure makes DSCR financing a natural fit for self-employed investors, business owners whose tax returns don't reflect their full cash flow, and anyone building a rental portfolio who'd rather qualify on the deal than re-prove their income every time. Because every lender sets its own DSCR requirements, property-type rules, and reserve expectations, comparing options across our network of 20+ wholesale lenders is especially valuable on this loan type.

DSCR vs. Conventional Investment Loans

Qualify on the Deal, Not Your Tax Returns.

Conventional investment property loans typically qualify you using your personal income, employment history, and debt-to-income ratio, the same process as financing a primary residence. DSCR loans take a different approach, evaluating the property's own rental income against its housing payment instead.

For investors with strong properties but complex or lower reported personal income, that difference can open doors a conventional loan wouldn't. We'll help you compare both paths to see which actually fits your situation.
