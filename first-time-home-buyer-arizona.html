First-Time Home Buyer Programs in Arizona

Down payment assistance and flexible financing, explained clearly and compared across 20+ lenders, so you understand what's actually available before you commit to a program.

Apply in 5 mins Schedule a Consultation (623) 755-7528

"First-time buyer" doesn't always mean what it sounds like. Many programs define a first-time buyer as someone who hasn't owned a home within the past few years, rather than someone who has literally never owned property, and some programs carve out exceptions for veterans or buyers purchasing in certain targeted areas. That nuance matters, because it opens eligibility to more buyers than the name implies.

Arizona's first-time buyer landscape includes county-level programs, statewide options, and financing that pairs with FHA or conventional loans. Because availability and eligibility genuinely depend on which county you're buying in, and funding can change over time, we'll confirm current programs together rather than relying on figures that may already be outdated.
